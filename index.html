Drone & Missile Simulator (Unity)

Complete physics-based flight simulator, built end to end. A custom multithreaded aerodynamic solver (per-vertex forces, Unity Jobs and Burst) cuts frame cost from 13 ms to 2 ms (~6× faster). Autonomous drones trained with deep reinforcement learning (ML-Agents) and depth-camera input over 50M+ steps reach 95% navigation accuracy in dense forests, and the simulator generates missile telemetry datasets for ML research.

Unity Physics Simulation Aerodynamics ML-Agents C#

Samsung — Avatar Body Capture (Unity)

AI full-body avatar estimation for Samsung XR devices, trained on AMASS motion-capture data, predicting full-body animation in real time from only head and hand tracking. Benchmarked Sentis, ONNX Runtime and TensorFlow Lite across mobile XR devices, reaching sub-1 ms latency per prediction.

Unity Sentis ONNX Runtime TensorFlow Lite Deep-Learning

Geospatial platform for the archaeological site of Kythnos, funded by the Greek government. Streams photogrammetry as Cesium 3D Tiles through a REST/JSON backend, with an in-platform AI assistant and live museum metadata so curators can update exhibits dynamically.

Unity Cesium 3D Tiles AI Chatbot
